Every GitHub account or organisation with projects on Grainlify has a public organization page. It shows your repositories, their activity, your community links and what contributors say about working with you. You can edit the links; the reviews come from contributors.

## Find your page

Go to **Browse**, switch to **Organizations**, and choose your organisation.

The page shows:

- your organisation's picture and how many repositories it has on Grainlify;
- your community links, starting with GitHub;
- **Repositories**, **Stars**, **Contributors** and **Merged PRs** across your listed repositories, and your organisation's rank;
- a contribution calendar;
- your listed repositories, one click away;
- **Reviews** from contributors, with the average rating.

![An organization page, with the Edit links button under the name](shot:org-page-header?desktop "Your organization page")

## Edit your community links

**Edit links** shows only to people who own at least one project under that organisation on Grainlify. Anyone else sees the links without the button.

1. **Choose Edit links** under your organisation's name.
2. **Fill in the ones you want.** The **Edit community links** window has **Telegram**, **LinkedIn**, **WhatsApp**, **Twitter / X** and **Discord**. Enter a handle without the @, or paste a full link. For WhatsApp, enter a phone number, digits only. Leave a box empty to hide that link.

   ![The Edit community links window](shot:org-edit-links-modal?desktop "Edit community links")

3. **Choose Save links.** The page says **Community links updated.** and the icons update.

The GitHub link always points at your organisation on GitHub and can't be changed.

## Reviews and ratings

Contributors rate an organisation from one to five stars, with an optional comment. They appear under **Reviews**, newest first, and the average and count appear near the top of the page.

| Who | Can they review your organisation? |
| --- | --- |
| Someone with a pull request merged into one of your repositories on Grainlify | Yes, once. They can edit their review later. |
| Someone with no merged pull request in your repositories | No. They see **Get a pull request merged into** your organisation's name **to leave a review here.** |
| You, or anyone who owns a project under your organisation | No. Owners can't review their own organisation. |

![The Reviews section with ratings and comments](shot:org-reviews?desktop "Reviews")

The best way to earn good reviews is the work itself: clear issues, quick decisions on applications, and prompt reviews of pull requests.
