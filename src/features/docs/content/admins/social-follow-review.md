Contributors follow Grainlify on LinkedIn and X and submit a screenshot of each, together. Approving a submission grants eligibility for the [Founding Contributor Pool](/docs/rewards). You review them in **Social follow review**, its own entry in the admin sidebar. One decision covers both screenshots.

## Filter the queue

Choose **Pending**, **Approved** or **All** above the list. **Pending** is the default. Each row shows the contributor's login, the date they submitted and the status. A decided row also shows the reason, if one was given, and who decided it and when. Use **Previous** and **Next** when there is more than one page.

![Social Follow Review on the Pending filter, with the selection bar and several submissions](shot:admin-follow-queue "The pending queue")

## View the proofs

Select a contributor's name to open their row. The LinkedIn and X screenshots appear side by side. Select a screenshot to open it full size in a new tab. One row is open at a time.

![An open submission showing the LinkedIn and X screenshots side by side](shot:admin-follow-proofs "Both proofs, one decision")

## Approve

Choose **Approve both**. The contributor is notified that their follow proof was approved and that they are eligible for the Founding Contributor Pool.

## Reject with a reason

1. **Choose Reject.** The **Reject this proof** window opens.
2. **Pick a reason.** The options are **X proof doesn't show a follow**, **LinkedIn proof doesn't show a follow**, **Screenshot unreadable or wrong image**, **Wrong account followed**, **Duplicate submission** and **Other**.
3. **Add a note.** It is optional for every reason except **Other**, where the field becomes **What was wrong?** and is required.
4. **Choose Confirm rejection.**

![The Reject this proof window with a reason selected](shot:admin-follow-reject-modal "Rejecting needs a reason")

The contributor is notified with the reason and your note, and told they can upload new screenshots and submit again.

## Revoke eligibility

Approved submissions have **Revoke eligibility** instead. Use it when someone no longer qualifies, for example because they unfollowed. Write the reason, which the contributor sees, and choose **Confirm revocation**. The submission and both screenshots are kept; only the status changes. The contributor is told why and that they can submit new proof to become eligible again.

## Approve several at once

1. **Stay on Pending.** Tick the rows you have checked, or tick **Select all on this page**.
2. **Choose Approve selected.** The button shows how many you ticked.
3. **Check the count and confirm.** The **Approve selected submissions** window says how many will be approved. Choose the **Approve** button with that number on it, for example **Approve 3**.

![The Approve selected submissions confirmation](shot:admin-follow-bulk-confirm "Check the count before you confirm")

The result tells you how many were approved, skipped and failed. A skipped row was already decided since the page loaded, so nothing was overwritten. Failed rows stay ticked so you can retry them.

Selection only ever covers the rows on the page you are looking at. Changing the filter or the page clears your ticks.

## The approval cap

At most 300 submissions can be approved at any one time. Only current approvals count, so revoking one frees a place. When the cap is reached, approving is refused with "The Founding Contributor Pool is full" and the submission is left as it was. In a bulk approval, rows refused by the cap are counted as skipped. Rejecting and revoking always work.
