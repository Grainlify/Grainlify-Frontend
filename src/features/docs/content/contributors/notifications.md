Grainlify tells you when something happens that concerns you: an application is received or decided, a pull request is merged, a follow proof is reviewed, a referral completes. This page covers the bell, the Notifications page, and how to choose what you get.

## The bell

The bell sits at the top of every dashboard page, with a count of unread notifications (up to **99+**). The count refreshes about once a minute. On a phone, the bell is in the menu.

1. **Open the bell.** It lists your ten newest notifications. Unread ones are highlighted.

   ![The notifications dropdown open, with unread notifications highlighted](shot:notifications-bell?desktop "The bell, open")

2. **Choose a notification.** It's marked as read, and the Notifications page opens with that notification highlighted.
3. **Or follow its link.** Under each notification, beside its time, a link takes you to what it's about, such as **Go to the issue**, **Go to the project** or **Go to rewards**.

**Mark all read** clears every unread notification at once. **See all notifications** opens the full page. Once you have more than eight notifications, **All** and **Unread** buttons let you narrow the list.

## The Notifications page

Choose **See all notifications** in the bell to open it. Notifications are grouped under **Today**, **This week** and **Earlier**, and the heading shows how many are unread.

![The Notifications page, grouped by date, with the All and Unread toggle and search](shot:notifications-page "The Notifications page")

- **Choose a notification** to mark it as read.
- **Mark all as read** marks everything read.
- **Load older** fetches the next set.
- Once you have more than eight notifications, **All** and **Unread** narrow the list, and **Search notifications** matches the title and text of the ones loaded.

## Choose what you get

Open the menu under your picture, choose **Settings**, then **Notifications**.

![Notification Preferences, with In-App and Email switches for each type](shot:settings-notifications "Settings → Notifications")

1. **Find the type.** There's one row per type of notification, grouped under **Contributor**, **Bounties**, **GrainHack**, **Maintainer** and **Other**, each with a short description.
2. **Set its switches.** Each row has an **In-App** switch and an **Email** switch. **Enable all** and **Disable all** change every row at once.
3. **Choose Save.** Nothing changes until you do. You'll see **Notification preferences saved.**

Every type is switched on for both until you change it. Turning **In-App** off for a type stops new notifications of that type reaching the bell and the page. Ones you already have stay.

> [!NOTE]
> Grainlify doesn't currently hold an email address for your account, so notification emails aren't being sent. Your notifications arrive in the app. Your **Email** choices are saved all the same.

For the full list of types and what triggers each, see [Notification types](/docs/reference/notification-types).
