Following Grainlify on LinkedIn and X is required to be eligible for the [Founding Contributor Pool](/docs/rewards). It earns no shares by itself: it's a requirement, not a payment. You send a screenshot for each platform, both together, and a person reviews them.

## Submit your proof

1. **Open the Rewards tab.** Open the menu under your picture, top right, choose **Settings**, then **Rewards**. The **Social Follow** card is below your pool position.
2. **Follow Grainlify on both platforms.** Choose **Follow** next to **LinkedIn**, and next to **X**. Each opens Grainlify's page in a new tab.
3. **Take a screenshot of each.** It should show the Grainlify account and that your account follows it, such as a "Following" button while you're signed in.
4. **Choose both screenshots.** Choose **Choose screenshot** on each row. Use a PNG, JPG, GIF or WEBP image under 5MB. The row then says **Screenshot ready**, and **Replace** swaps it.

   ![The Social Follow card with a screenshot ready for both platforms](shot:social-follow-ready?desktop "Both screenshots ready")

5. **Choose Submit both for review.** The button only works once both rows have a screenshot. You'll see **Both screenshots submitted for review.**

There's no way to submit one platform on its own. Both screenshots are approved or rejected together, as one decision.

## What the status means

After you submit, a badge on the card shows where your proof stands.

| Badge | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pending review** | Waiting for a person to review it. | Nothing. If you need to, you can replace it by submitting two new screenshots. |
| **Eligible** | Approved. You meet the follow requirement. | Nothing. The upload form is hidden. |
| **Not approved** | Rejected. The reason is shown under the badge. | Fix what the reason says, choose two new screenshots and submit again. There's no limit on attempts. |
| **Eligibility withdrawn** | An earlier approval was withdrawn. The reason is shown. | Follow again and submit new screenshots to become eligible again. |

![A proof waiting for review](shot:social-follow-pending "Pending review")

![A rejected proof with the reason shown](shot:social-follow-rejected "Not approved")

You also get a notification for each decision: **Social follow approved**, **Social follow proof needs another look**, or **Social follow eligibility withdrawn**.

> [!NOTE]
> At most 300 follow proofs can be approved at any one time. If that many are already approved, a new submission can't be approved and stays as it is.

## Approval can be withdrawn

An approval isn't permanent. If it's withdrawn, the card says so and shows why.

![A withdrawn approval with the reason and what to do next](shot:social-follow-revoked "Eligibility withdrawn")

Pool eligibility is checked when the pool is shared out. What's checked is that your approval hasn't been withdrawn. LinkedIn and X don't offer a way to confirm a follow automatically, so it isn't a live check that you still follow on the day.

## A good screenshot

Make sure each one clearly shows:

- The Grainlify account you're following.
- That your own account is the one following it, for example a visible "Following" button.

If you hold a place in the pool without an approved proof, your position card says **You're not currently eligible to receive a share.** Your place and multiplier aren't affected. Getting your proof approved fixes it.
