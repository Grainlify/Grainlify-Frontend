Share your referral link to bring people to Grainlify. When someone joins through it and verifies their identity, your referral completes and counts toward your shares in the [Founding Contributor Pool](/docs/rewards).

## Find your link

1. **Open the Referrals tab.** Open the menu under your picture, top right, choose **Settings**, then **Referrals**.
2. **Copy your link.** The tab shows **Your referral code** and **Your referral link**, each with a copy button. Share the link wherever you like.

   ![The Referrals tab with your code, your link and your counts](shot:referrals-tab "Settings → Referrals")

## When a referral counts

A referral is recorded when someone opens your link and then creates a new Grainlify account.

- **A click counts for 30 days.** If they sign up more than 30 days after clicking, or sign up without using your link, the referral doesn't count.
- **It must be a new account.** Someone who already has a Grainlify account and signs in through your link isn't referred.
- **You can't refer yourself.**

The referral then shows as **Pending**. It becomes **Completed** when the person verifies their identity, and you get a notification, **Someone you referred is verified**.

Verification is the only thing that completes a referral. There's no need for a merged pull request, a minimum number of contributions, or a deadline for them to verify. A completed referral with no merged work yet is normal.

## What a referral earns

| When | Shares for you |
| --- | --- |
| Someone you referred verifies their identity | For each referral, up to a lifetime cap |
| Someone you referred gets a pull request merged and accepted in a GrainHack event | For each pull request, no cap |

**The cap.** It limits the total shares you can earn from referrals that only verify. It's a ceiling on your total, not a filter on each referral, so the referral that crosses it still earns the part that fits. After that, new verifications earn you nothing, but merged work from people you referred still does, without limit.

The number of shares for each, and the cap, are settings. The live values are in **My GrainHack → Rules**, under **Founding pool**.

> [!WARNING]
> Referral shares are recorded whether or not you're eligible, but only eligible members receive anything when the pool is shared out. That needs an approved [social follow](/docs/contributors/social-follow), a verified identity, and a registered payout address. Without them, your referral shares pay nothing.

A share isn't a fixed amount of money: its value isn't known until the pool is shared out. See [Founding Contributor Pool](/docs/rewards) for the arithmetic.

## Track your referrals

The Referrals tab counts **Total Referred**, **Pending** and **Completed**. It doesn't show shares or an amount, because a share's value is only known when the pool is divided.
