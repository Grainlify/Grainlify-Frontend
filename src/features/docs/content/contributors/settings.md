Settings is where you manage your account: your profile, what you're told about, your referrals, your Founding Contributor Pool place and where payouts go. This page gives each tab a short tour and links to the page that covers it in full.

## Open Settings

Open the menu under your picture, top right, and choose **Settings**. The tabs run across the top, and **Profile** opens first. The tab you're on is kept in the address, so reloading the page keeps you there.

![The menu under your picture, with Public Profile, Settings and Logout](shot:settings-menu?desktop "Opening Settings")

## Profile

Your picture, name, location, website, bio and contact handles, and the button that refreshes your username and picture from GitHub. Only some of these appear on your public profile.

See [Edit your profile](/docs/contributors/edit-profile).

## Notifications

The email address notifications are sent to, with a switch that turns off all email. Then a row for each type of notification, with an **In-App** and an **Email** switch, plus **Enable all** and **Disable all**. Choose **Save** to keep your changes.

See [Notifications](/docs/contributors/notifications).

## Referrals

Your referral code and link, with copy buttons, and counts of how many people you've referred and how many are pending or completed.

See [Referrals](/docs/contributors/referrals).

## Rewards

Two cards. The first shows your place in the Founding Contributor Pool, if you have one, and whether you're eligible. The second, **Social Follow**, is where you submit your LinkedIn and X screenshots and see their status.

See [Founding Contributor Pool](/docs/rewards) and [Social follow](/docs/contributors/social-follow).

## Payout Preferences

Where GrainHack and Founding Contributor Pool payouts are sent. From the top:

- Any payouts that have been published for you.
- The payout readiness card, when you have something to do or were left out of a payout.
- Your Aptos payout address, registered with Petra, and the optional payout contact field.

See [How payouts work](/docs/contributors/payouts), [Register your payout address](/docs/contributors/payout-address) and [Payout readiness](/docs/contributors/payout-readiness). Bounties are paid to a Solana wallet you link elsewhere: see [Link your Solana wallet](/docs/contributors/link-solana-wallet).

## Terms and Conditions

The terms of using Grainlify, with **On this page** links to jump to each section.

At the bottom, choose **Accept** to acknowledge them. The card then says **Terms accepted**. Your acceptance is remembered on this device only, so another browser shows **Accept** again.

![The Accept Terms card at the end of the terms](shot:settings-terms-accept "Accepting the terms")
