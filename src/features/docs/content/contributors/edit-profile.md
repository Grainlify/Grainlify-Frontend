You edit your profile in **Settings → Profile**: your picture, your details and your contact handles. Your username and email come from GitHub, and you change those on GitHub. Open the menu under your picture, top right, choose **Settings**, and the **Profile** tab opens first.

## Your GitHub account

The **GitHub account** card shows your GitHub username and email address. Grainlify reads them from GitHub rather than letting you type them.

1. **Choose Edit.** It opens your profile settings on GitHub in a new tab. Make your change there.
2. **Come back and choose Resync.** Grainlify fetches your username and picture from GitHub again. You'll see **GitHub profile synced successfully!**

   ![The GitHub account card with the Resync and Edit buttons, and the Profile Picture card](shot:settings-profile-github "GitHub account and profile picture")

## Your profile picture

Your GitHub picture is used until you choose another.

1. **Choose Update** on the **Profile Picture** card, and pick an image. It can be SVG, PNG, JPG or GIF, up to 5MB.
2. **Choose Save Picture.** Until you do, the new picture is only a preview. You'll see **Profile picture updated successfully!**

The picture you save is the one other people see on your profile page.

## Your details

| Field | Shown on your public profile? |
| --- | --- |
| **First Name**, **Last Name** | No |
| **Location** | No |
| **Website** | Yes |
| **Bio** | Yes |

If you leave a field empty, it's filled in from your GitHub profile where GitHub has a value.

## Contact Information

Add your handles for **Telegram**, **LinkedIn**, **WhatsApp**, **Twitter** and **Discord**. Enter only the handle: no links, and no @. Each one you add appears as an icon on your public profile.

![The details fields and the Contact Information card](shot:settings-profile-details "Your details and contact handles")

## Save your changes

Choose **Save** at the bottom of the tab. It's only available once you've changed something. You'll see **Profile updated successfully!**

The picture is saved separately, with **Save Picture**. **Save** doesn't save a new picture.

> [!NOTE]
> Your profile page shows your GitHub username, not the name you enter here. See [Your public profile](/docs/contributors/profile) for everything that's shown.
