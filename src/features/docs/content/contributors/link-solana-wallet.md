Bounties are paid in USDC or $ANSEM on Solana, to the wallet linked to your GitHub account. You link it once by signing a message. Signing costs nothing and sends no transaction.

![Link your Solana wallet](video:contributors/link-solana-wallet "1:17")

## Before you start

- A Grainlify account, signed in with GitHub.
- A Solana wallet: Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. A wallet app on your phone is enough.

## Link from a computer

1. **Open the wallet page.** In **Bounties**, choose **Link your wallet**, or go straight to [grainlify.com/bounties/link](/bounties/link).
2. **Choose your wallet.** Wallets installed in this browser are listed under **Found in this browser**, marked **Detected**. Choose yours and approve the connection in the wallet.

   ![The wallet page, with Phantom detected in this browser](shot:walletlink-connect "Step 1 of 3: Connect")

3. **Check the message, then sign it.** The page shows exactly what your wallet will ask you to sign: your GitHub login, the wallet address, a one-time code, and an expiry ten minutes away. If it matches, choose **Sign message** and approve it in your wallet.

   ![The message your wallet will show, with the Sign message button](shot:walletlink-sign "Step 2 of 3: Sign")

4. **You're done.** The page says **Wallet linked** and shows the full address. Bounties you win are paid there.

   ![The wallet page after linking, showing the linked address](shot:walletlink-linked "Step 3 of 3: Linked")

> [!NOTE]
> Signing is free. It is not a transaction and it cannot move funds. Grainlify never asks for your recovery phrase. If a page does, close it.

## Link from a phone

A phone browser can't see your wallet app, so the page offers **Open in Phantom** and **Open in Solflare** instead. Each one reopens the page inside that wallet's own browser, where the steps above work the same way. On Android, **Use a wallet app on this phone** connects without leaving your browser.

## Change your wallet

Link a different wallet the same way. It replaces the one on file for your GitHub account, and later payouts go to the new address.

## If something goes wrong

> [!WARNING]
> **"That wallet is already linked to another GitHub account."** A wallet can belong to one GitHub account. Use a different wallet, or unlink it from the other account first.

- **You declined the signature.** Nothing was linked. Choose **Sign message** again when you're ready.
- **"Your Grainlify account has no GitHub account attached."** Bounties are paid by GitHub account. Sign in with GitHub and try again.
- **Still stuck?** [Get help](/support) and tell us which wallet you used.
