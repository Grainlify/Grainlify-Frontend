The payout readiness card tells you whether you still need to do anything before a payout can reach you. It matters because a payout can be published at any time, and one published before you've registered a payout address leaves you out.

## Where to find it

Open the menu under your picture, top right, choose **Settings**, then **Payout Preferences**. The card sits above your payout address.

It appears only when there's something to say: you hold a position in the [Founding Contributor Pool](/docs/rewards), or you were left out of a payout that has been published. If neither is true, there's no card and nothing to do.

## What each state means

| The card says | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Register a payout address** | You hold a pool position, so a payout will be owed to you, but you have no payout address yet. | [Register your payout address](/docs/contributors/payout-address) now. |
| **You're set up for payouts** | Your payout address is registered. Nothing is owed to you yet. | Nothing. Anything published later appears on this tab. |
| **You were left out of a payout that has already been published** | A payout was published without a share for you. | Contact us, and register an address if you still don't have one. |
| No card | You hold no pool position and haven't been left out of anything. | Nothing. |

### Register a payout address

![The readiness card asking you to register a payout address](shot:readiness-register-now "Register a payout address")

There's no date on this, and that's deliberate: nobody knows exactly when the next payout will be published. Registering takes about a minute and costs nothing, and once it's done you can't miss a payout for lack of an address.

### You're set up for payouts

![The readiness card saying you're set up for payouts](shot:readiness-ready "You're set up for payouts")

This is the state you want. You don't need to check back: when something is published for you, it appears on the **Payout Preferences** tab.

### You were left out of a published payout

![The readiness card saying you were left out of a published payout](shot:readiness-excluded "Left out of a published payout")

The card gives the reason. Either the payout was published before you had a verified payout address, or it was published without a GitHub account linked to you. A published payout can't be edited, so this one can't be corrected in place.

> [!WARNING]
> It isn't automatically recoverable, but it isn't automatically lost either. [Contact us](/support) and ask, rather than assuming the money is gone.

If the card says **Register a payout address below so this cannot happen again**, do that too. If your address is already registered, the card says future payouts will include you.

## If the card can't load

While it checks, the card says **Checking whether you need to do anything…** If the check fails, it says **Couldn't check your payout status.** A failed check doesn't mean there's nothing to do. Reload the page, and [contact us](/support) if it keeps failing.

## GrainHack payouts

The card's reminder to register comes from your pool position. If you take part in GrainHack, register a payout address anyway: a GrainHack payout is locked to the address you had when it was published, in the same way. See [How payouts work](/docs/contributors/payouts).
