Grainlify pays through three programmes, and they don't all pay the same way. This page tells you which programme pays where, and what you need to set up before any money can reach you.

## Which programme pays how

| Programme | Paid in | Paid to | What you set up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Bounties** | USDC or $ANSEM on Solana | The Solana wallet linked to your GitHub account | [Link your Solana wallet](/docs/contributors/link-solana-wallet) |
| **GrainHack** | USDC on Aptos | Your registered payout address | [Register your payout address](/docs/contributors/payout-address) |
| **Founding Contributor Pool** | USDC on Aptos | Your registered payout address | [Register your payout address](/docs/contributors/payout-address), plus the pool's own [eligibility](/docs/rewards) |

The two setups are separate. Your Solana wallet doesn't receive GrainHack or pool payouts, and your Aptos payout address doesn't receive bounties. If you take part in both, set up both.

## Bounties

A bounty is paid once its pull request is merged and a person approves the payout. It goes to the Solana wallet linked to your GitHub account. If you link a different wallet, later payouts go to the new one.

Every bounty payout is listed with its proof on the ledger. See [From pull request to payment](/docs/contributors/bounty-payment) and [The bounty ledger](/docs/contributors/bounty-ledger).

## GrainHack and the Founding Contributor Pool

These two are paid the same way.

**Amounts are decided once, at the end.** A GrainHack event's amounts are worked out after its appeals close. The pool's are worked out when it's shared out. Until then nobody, Grainlify included, can say what you'll receive, so the app never shows you a figure in advance.

**The payout is published on Aptos.** Each person's share is tied to the payout address registered on their account at that moment.

**It then appears in Payout Preferences.** Open the menu under your picture, top right, choose **Settings**, then **Payout Preferences**. A published payout shows its amount and a **View the published payout** link.

![Payout Preferences with the readiness card and a verified payout address](shot:payouts-payout-tab "Settings → Payout Preferences")

> [!WARNING]
> A payout is locked to the address that was registered when it was published, and it can't be moved afterwards. If you have no payout address at that moment, you're left out of that payout, and a published payout can't be edited. Register your address early. It takes about a minute and costs nothing.

Both programmes also require your identity to be verified when a payout is published.

## Changing your payout address

You can register a different Aptos address at any time. Payouts published after the change go to the new address. Payouts already published stay locked to the address they were published with, so choose a wallet you'll still have months from now.

## Check whether you're ready

The **Payout Preferences** tab tells you where you stand:

- The [payout readiness card](/docs/contributors/payout-readiness) says whether you still need to do anything, and warns you if you were left out of a published payout.
- The payout address card shows the address payouts go to and the date you verified it.

The app shows the current state rather than a promise. If you're registered and nothing has been published for you yet, the readiness card says **You're set up for payouts** and that nothing is owed to you yet.
