# From pull request to payment

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: contributor. Start: /dashboard?tab=bounties

## 1
Say: [upbeat] You've won a bounty's draw. Here's how your work becomes a payment.
Do: The Bounties page is open, showing the open bounties list.

## 2
Say: [calm] Open a pull request on GitHub with closes, then the issue number, in its description. [serious] Do it before your assignment runs out. The rules page shows how long a winner has.
Do: Go to /bounties/rules and scroll to the **Assignment** section, holding on **assignment_stale_hours**.

## 3
Say: [matter-of-fact] The agent posts an advisory review on your pull request. It's advice ONLY. It can't approve anything or trigger a payment. [pause] On the Bounties page, the bounty now shows PR in review.
Do: Go back to /dashboard?tab=bounties and zoom in on the row marked **PR in review**.

## 4
Say: [serious] A maintainer decides whether to merge. They need write access, and it can't be you. Self-merged pull requests are not paid.
Do: Scroll to the **How to claim a bounty** card and hold on its last line of rules.

## 5
Say: [confident] On merge, the agent runs a fixed list of checks in plain code... Among them: you were the person drawn, the pull request closes the issue, and your wallet is linked. When every check passes, the bounty shows awaiting approval.
Do: Zoom in on the row marked **Awaiting approval**.

## 6
Say: [pleased] Then a person approves the payout. The agent can't. Once it's sent, the bounty moves to paid, with a link to the transaction.
Do: Scroll to **Paid** and hover the **transaction** link on a paid bounty.

## 7
Say: [warmly] Every payout also appears on the bounty ledger, with its proof.
Do: Click **Open the ledger**, then choose **Payouts**.
