# GrainHack for maintainers

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: maintainer. Start: /dashboard?tab=maintainers&view=maintainer&subtab=Issues

## 1
Say: [warmly] If your project is taking part in a GrainHack, you choose which issues go in. You do it with a label on GitHub.
Do: Show the **Issues** tab. Cut briefly to a title card: "On GitHub: add the event's label to the issue."

## 2
Say: [matter-of-fact] Once Grainlify picks up the label, the issue shows a GrainHack panel... It stays pending, and nobody can apply, until two things are filled in.
Do: Click the issue "Document the CLI flags" in tidewater-labs/ledgerline (#242). The panel headed **GrainHack:** shows pending, with **Missing before this publishes** listing **Acceptance criteria** and **Difficulty tier**.

## 3
Say: [thoughtful] First, the acceptance criteria: what must be true for a pull request to satisfy this issue. The work is checked against them, so be SPECIFIC.
Do: Click **Acceptance criteria** and type "Every flag in --help is documented in docs/cli.md with an example."

## 4
Say: [calm] Then the difficulty tier. The draw uses it when it weighs applicants.
Do: Open **Difficulty tier** and choose **Standard**.

## 5
Say: [pleased] Save, and the issue is published. Contributors can now apply for it.
Do: Click **Save**. The toast says **Saved - issue is now published to GrainHack.** The panel shows published.

## 6
Say: [firm] You don't pick who works on it. A weighted draw does, so don't assign GrainHack issues yourself on GitHub.
Do: Stay on the issue with the published panel in view. Cut briefly to a title card: "The draw picks who works on it."

## 7
Say: [confident] Maintainers have their own pool, scored on new contributors, review speed, an established repository and clear issues. [pause] Not on how many issues you open or merge. The settings are on the Rules page.
Do: Click **My GrainHack** in the rail, then **Rules**. Scroll to **Maintainer pool**.
