# Get help

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: contributor (mira-dev) with four earlier support reports in the fixture. One image file ready to upload: search-bug.png. Start: /dashboard?tab=discover

## 1
Say: [warmly] If something's broken or you're stuck, use Get help. It's at the bottom of the rail.
Do: Hover **Get help** at the bottom of the rail, then click it. The **Get help** page opens.

## 2
Say: [calm] First, choose what it's about: a bug, verification, an idea, help, or something else. [pause] Your choice decides who sees your report.
Do: Hover across **Bug**, **Verification**, **Idea**, **Help** and **Other** under **What's this about?**

## 3
Say: [serious] Verification questions go to the team only, NEVER to a public channel. Please don't include ID numbers or photos of documents. Nobody needs them to help you.
Do: Click **Verification**. Zoom on the note that begins "Handled privately".

## 4
Say: [matter-of-fact] For every other category, your message and the page you were on are also posted in Grainlify's public Telegram group, without your name. Screenshots go to the team only.
Do: Click **Bug**.

## 5
Say: [encouraging] Describe what happened, add a screenshot if it helps, and choose Send.
Do: Type "Search shows no results for a project I can see on Browse." in **Tell us more**. Click **Attach a screenshot** and pick search-bug.png. Click **Send**.

## 6
Say: [reassuring] Your report is saved before it's delivered, so it isn't lost if a channel is down... Choose Done to clear the form.
Do: The confirmation **Thanks - we've got it** shows. Click **Done**.

## 7
Say: [gentle] Below the form, Your reports lists everything you've sent, with an ID to quote if you follow up.
Do: Scroll to **Your reports**. Zoom on the newest report, its **Received** label and its ID.

## 8
Say: [friendly] Um, you don't need to be signed in. There's a Get help link on the sign-in page too.
Do: Cut to /signin signed out. Hover the **Get help** link at the bottom of the sign-in card.
