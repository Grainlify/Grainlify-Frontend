# Link your Solana wallet

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: contributor, no wallet linked yet, Phantom detected in the browser. Start: /dashboard?tab=bounties

## 1
Say: [reassuring] Bounties are paid to a Solana wallet linked to your GitHub account. You link it once, by signing a message. [pause] Signing is free, and it doesn't send a transaction.
Do: The Bounties page is open. Hold on the wallet card, which says **No wallet linked**.

## 2
Say: [friendly] On the Bounties page, choose Link your wallet.
Do: Click **Link your wallet**. The page "Link a wallet to get paid" loads, on step 1, **Connect**.

## 3
Say: [calm] Wallets installed in this browser are listed first, marked detected. Choose yours, and approve the connection in the wallet.
Do: Hover **Phantom**, with its **Detected** badge, then click it. The page moves to **Sign to confirm**.

## 4
Say: [careful] Before you sign, the page shows exactly what your wallet will ask you to sign: your GitHub login, the wallet address, a one-time code, and an expiry ten minutes away.
Do: Zoom in on the message under "Your wallet will show exactly this:".

## 5
Say: [confident] If it matches, choose Sign message and approve it in your wallet. [serious] Grainlify NEVER asks for your recovery phrase. If a page ever does, close it.
Do: Point at the line "Free. No transaction. Valid once, for 10 minutes. We never ask for a recovery phrase." Click **Sign message**.

## 6
Say: [pleased] That's it. The page says wallet linked and shows the full address. Bounties you win are paid there.
Do: The **Wallet linked** heading appears, with **Linked just now** and the address.

## 7
Say: [warmly] On a phone, the page offers to open itself inside Phantom or Solflare, and the steps are the same... To change wallets later, link a new one. It replaces the old one.
Do: Click **Back to Bounties**. The wallet card now says **Wallet linked**, with **Copy** and **Change**.
