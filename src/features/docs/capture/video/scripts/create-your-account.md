# Create your account

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: contributor. Start: /signin

## 1
Say: [warmly] You sign in to Grainlify with your GitHub account. [chuckles] There's no form to fill in and no password. The first time you sign in, your account is created.
Do: Show the sign-in page with the **Sign in with GitHub** button. Move the pointer onto the button without clicking.

## 2
Say: [friendly] Uh, signing up and signing in are the same thing. The sign-up page's button does exactly the same as this one.
Do: Navigate to /signup and point at **Sign up with GitHub** without clicking. Then navigate back to /signin.

## 3
Say: [reassuring] When you choose Sign in with GitHub, GitHub asks you to approve Grainlify. It asks to read your profile, your email addresses and your organisation memberships, and for access to your public repositories... It NEVER asks for your private repositories.
Do: Stay on /signin with the pointer on **Sign in with GitHub**; do not click it, and do not record GitHub's own page. Cut to the next segment.

## 4
Say: [pleased] Once you approve, you're back on Grainlify, signed in, on Discover.
Do: Start on /dashboard as mira-dev on a first visit, on a computer, so the product tour opens. Hold on **Discover** as the tour's first step appears.

## 5
Say: [upbeat] On your first visit, a short tour points out the main parts of the app. Move through it with Next, or leave any time.
Do: Click **Next** twice, pausing on the **Discover** and **Browse** steps.

## 6
Say: [calm] You can skip the rest with the Escape key. [pause] The tour only appears once, and not on a phone.
Do: Press Esc to close the tour. Do not show the tour's last step.

## 7
Say: [matter-of-fact] To sign out, open the menu under your picture at the top right, and choose Logout. Coming back is one click if you're still signed in to GitHub.
Do: Click the picture and username at the top right to open the menu. Point at **Logout** without clicking, then press Esc to close the menu.
