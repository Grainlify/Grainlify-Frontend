# The bounty ledger

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: contributor. Start: /dashboard?tab=bounties

## 1
Say: [calm] The bounty ledger is the record of the programme's money: bounties posted, the model calls the agent pays for, the checks on merged pull requests, and payouts. [pause] EVERY row links to its proof.
Do: Click **Open the ledger**. The **Bounty Ledger** header loads.

## 2
Say: [matter-of-fact] The banner says whether bounties are paying on mainnet yet. Below it are the totals: creator fees in, what the agent has spent on model calls, bounties paid, and how many model calls it has made.
Do: Scroll to the **All time** tiles and pan across them.

## 3
Say: [reassuring] The inference budget shows the agent's spending against each phase. The agent and its payment signer each stop at a fixed ceiling on their own.
Do: Pan to **Inference budget**.

## 4
Say: [confident] The receipt chain follows the most recently paid bounty from start to finish: posted, priced, reviewed, checked, and paid.
Do: Pan to **Receipt chain · latest paid bounty** and scroll down its rows.

## 5
Say: [friendly] Filter the events by period and by type... Here are the payouts from the last thirty days.
Do: Click **30 days**, then **Payouts**.

## 6
Say: [calm] Each row has a time in U T C, what happened, the amount, and a proof link: the issue, the pull request, or the transaction.
Do: Hover a row's link in the **Proof** column.

## 7
Say: [measured] Gate passed and gate refused are the payout checks on a merged pull request... Rows marked test are the devnet run, paid in test tokens with no value.
Do: Click **All time**, then **Bounties**. Point at a **Gate passed** row, then at an event marked "test".
