# Bounties on your repositories

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: maintainer. Start: /dashboard?tab=bounties

## 1
Say: [calm] On some repositories, Grainlify Bounties pay contributors for fixing selected issues. Grainlify switches bounties on for a repository; there's no setting for it in the app.
Do: Show the **Bounties** page, signed in as owen-maintains, scrolled to the top.

## 2
Say: [matter-of-fact] The top of this page says whether bounties are paying on mainnet yet. Below it are the open bounties, with a window for applications.
Do: Point at the status notice at the top, then scroll to the open bounties list.

## 3
Say: [friendly] You don't choose who gets a bounty. Contributors apply here, and one is drawn when the window closes. The page shows who holds it.
Do: Point at the bounty on owen-labs/ledgerkit showing **Assigned to** mira-dev.

## 4
Say: [reassuring] When a pull request closes the bounty issue, the bounty agent posts an advisory review on GitHub. It's a comment only. It NEVER approves the pull request and never blocks a merge.
Do: Cut to a title card: "On GitHub: Advisory review for bounty on #12: Looks complete."

## 5
Say: [confident] You review and merge as usual... Your merge is the gate: nothing is paid without it, and the merge must come from a maintainer who didn't write the pull request.
Do: Keep the title card, adding a second line: "You merge on GitHub."

## 6
Say: [thoughtful] After the merge, the agent posts a checklist saying whether the payout is ready for approval. A person approves every payout, and the agent comments again when it's paid.
Do: Cut back to the **Bounties** page. Click **Open the ledger** and show the ledger.

## 7
Say: [serious] One rule to know: anyone with write access or more to a repository can't win its bounties. [pause] That includes you.
Do: Go back to the **Bounties** page and scroll to the rules line under **How to claim a bounty**.
