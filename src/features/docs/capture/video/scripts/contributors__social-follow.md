# Social follow

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: contributor (mira-dev) holding a Founding Contributor Pool place, no follow proof submitted yet. Two image files ready to upload: linkedin-follow.png and x-follow.png. Later segments cut to the fixtures where the proof is rejected and where it is withdrawn. Start: /dashboard?tab=settings&subtab=rewards

Framing for the whole video: keep the Settings tab bar out of frame.

## 1
Say: [calm] To be eligible for the Founding Contributor Pool, you follow Grainlify on LinkedIn and X and send a screenshot of each. [pause] Following earns no shares by itself. It's a requirement, not a payment.
Do: The Rewards tab is open. Hold on the position card and the **Social Follow** card below it.

## 2
Say: [reassuring] If you hold a place without an approved follow, the card at the top says you're not eligible yet. Your place and multiplier aren't affected.
Do: Zoom on "You're not currently eligible to receive a share." and the sentence about your position being permanent.

## 3
Say: [friendly] In the Social Follow card, choose Follow next to each platform, follow Grainlify there, and take a screenshot that shows your account following it.
Do: Hover **Follow** next to **LinkedIn**, then next to **X**. Don't click.

## 4
Say: [matter-of-fact] Choose a screenshot for each platform. Both are sent together... There's no way to send one on its own.
Do: Click **Choose screenshot** on the LinkedIn row and pick linkedin-follow.png. The row shows **Screenshot ready**. Do the same on the X row with x-follow.png.

## 5
Say: [confident] When both are ready, choose Submit both for review. A person reviews them, and the badge says pending review until they do.
Do: Click **Submit both for review**. The toast "Both screenshots submitted for review." appears and the badge **Pending review** shows.

## 6
Say: [gentle] BOTH screenshots are approved or rejected together. If yours aren't approved, the reason is shown, and you can choose two new screenshots and try again.
Do: Cut to the rejected fixture. Zoom on the **Not approved** badge and the reason beneath it.

## 7
Say: [serious] An approval can also be withdrawn later, and the card tells you why. [pause] Eligibility is checked when the pool is shared out, so follow again and resubmit to become eligible again.
Do: Cut to the withdrawn fixture. Zoom on **Eligibility withdrawn**, the reason, and "You can follow again and submit new screenshots to become eligible."
