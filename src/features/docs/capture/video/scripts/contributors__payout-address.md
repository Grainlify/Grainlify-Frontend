# Register your payout address

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: contributor (mira-dev) holding a Founding Contributor Pool place, no payout address registered yet, Petra installed in the browser. Start: /dashboard?tab=settings&subtab=payout

Framing for the whole video: keep the Settings tab bar out of frame, and crop so only the readiness card and the "Register your payout address" card are visible. Nothing below that card may appear on screen.

## 1
Say: [calm] Payouts from GrainHack and the Founding Contributor Pool go to an Aptos address that you register once. [pause] You prove the address is yours by signing a message with the Petra wallet.
Do: Payout Preferences is open. Hold on the two cards: the readiness card and **Register your payout address**.

## 2
Say: [matter-of-fact] Open Settings from the menu under your picture, then Payout Preferences. Because you hold a place in the pool, the card at the top asks you to register an address before a payout is published.
Do: Zoom on the readiness card headed **Register a payout address**.

## 3
Say: [friendly] Choose Connect wallet and verify, and approve the connection in Petra.
Do: Click **Connect wallet and verify**. The button changes to **Waiting for your wallet…** Petra's connect prompt appears; approve it.

## 4
Say: [reassuring] Petra then asks you to sign a message. Signing costs no gas and moves no funds. It only proves that you control the address.
Do: Petra's signing prompt appears; approve it.

## 5
Say: [pleased] That's it. The card now says payout address verified, and shows the address, the date you verified it, and the network.
Do: The toast **Payout address verified.** appears and the card heading changes to **Payout address verified**. Zoom on the address and the "Verified" line.

## 6
Say: [serious] Choose a wallet you'll keep... A payout is locked to the address that was registered when it's published, and it CAN'T be moved afterwards.
Do: Zoom on the paragraph beginning "Choose a wallet you'll still have later."

## 7
Say: [warmly] Below the address, you can leave an email address for messages about your payouts. It's optional, and you can remove it at any time.
Do: Click the field under **How should we contact you about payouts?**, type "mira@example.com", click **Save**. The toast "We'll use this to tell you about payouts." appears.

## 8
Say: [upbeat] Reload the page, and the card at the top now says you're set up for payouts.
Do: Reload. The readiness card reads **You're set up for payouts**.
