# Verification review

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: admin. Start: /dashboard?tab=admin&view=admin

## 1
Say: [calm] When a contributor's identity verification needs a person's decision, they wait in Verification Review, on the Reviews page.
Do: The page opens on the **Admin Panel**. Scroll down to the **Verification Review** section so at least two queue rows are visible.

## 2
Say: [matter-of-fact] Each row is one contributor. In review means Didit has handed the session to us for a manual decision. [pause] Refused means Didit said no, and they may still need telling why.
Do: Move the pointer to the **In review** badge on one row, then to the **Refused** badge on another.

## 3
Say: [serious] The queue never shows the document. Match the row to the session in Didit by its session number, and decide it there first. Resetting here does NOT reach Didit.
Do: Move the pointer over the "Didit session #" line on the **Refused** row, then click the copy button next to its session id.

## 4
Say: [friendly] When you're ready, choose Send feedback and reset.
Do: Click **Send feedback & reset** on the **Refused** row. The **Reset** window opens with the contributor's login in its title.

## 5
Say: [thoughtful] Pick what they should fix. Every reason shows the exact message the contributor will read, and a reason marked suggested matches Didit's warnings.
Do: Scroll slowly through **What should they fix?**. Select the reason marked **suggested**, or **Photo of a screen, not the document** if none is.

## 6
Say: [gentle] Add a note if it helps them. Then record why you are resetting. That field is internal, and the contributor never sees it.
Do: In the field marked **(they read this)**, type "Please photograph the original card, not a scan." In **Why are you resetting?**, type "Screen capture flagged in Didit, declined there first."

## 7
Say: [pleased] Choose Send and reset. They can verify again straight away, and a notification tells them what to fix. The case leaves the queue.
Do: Click **Send & reset**. The confirmation "can verify again and has been told why." appears and the row disappears from the queue.
