# Maintainer quick start

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: maintainer. Start: /dashboard?tab=discover

## 1
Say: [warmly] Grainlify puts your project's issues in front of people looking for work, and gives you one place to decide who does it. [pause] Here's the whole path, from installing the app to your first assignment.
Do: Show Discover, signed in as owen-maintains. Hover over the role switcher in the header.

## 2
Say: [friendly] Your maintainer tools are in the same account. Switch to the maintainer view to open them.
Do: Click **MAINTAINER**. The Maintainers page opens on the **Dashboard** tab.

## 3
Say: [calm] To add a repository, open the repository selector and choose Add a repository. Grainlify explains what the GitHub App asks for, and sends you to GitHub to pick your repositories.
Do: Click **Select repositories**, then **Add a repository**. The **Install Grainlify GitHub App** window opens. Pause on it, then close it with **Cancel**. Cut to a title card: "On GitHub: pick your repositories and install."

## 4
Say: [matter-of-fact] GitHub brings you back to Discover... Switch to the maintainer view again, and a setup window opens for your new repository.
Do: Load /dashboard?github_app_installed=true (fixture with one repository needing setup). Click **MAINTAINER**. The **New Project Setup** window opens.

## 5
Say: [pleased] Add a short description, choose an ecosystem, and save. Your project is now listed for contributors.
Do: Type a description in **Description**, choose an ecosystem from **Select an ecosystem**, click **Save & Continue**. The window shows **Project details saved.** and closes.

## 6
Say: [reassuring] When people apply, you get a notification, and the issue shows its applicants in the Issues tab.
Do: Click the **Issues** tab. Click an issue showing "3 applicants".

## 7
Say: [confident] Expand an application to read the message, then choose Assign. Grainlify assigns them on GitHub, posts a comment, and lets them know.
Do: Click the arrow beside the first applicant to expand it. Click **Assign**. The application shows **Assigned**.

## 8
Say: [relaxed] Their pull request shows up in the Pull Requests tab. You review and merge it on GitHub, as you always do.
Do: Click the **Pull Requests** tab. Hover over an open pull request row.
