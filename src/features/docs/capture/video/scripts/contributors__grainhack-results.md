# Results, grading and appeals

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: contributor with published results and an open appeal window. Start: /dashboard?tab=my-grainhack&subtab=results

## 1
Say: [calm] When a GrainHack event publishes results, your grades appear in My GrainHack, under My results. [pause] You won't get a notification, so check here.
Do: **My results** is open, showing a graded pull request.

## 2
Say: [matter-of-fact] Each result shows its grade: exceptional, substantial, accepted, or not accepted.
Do: Zoom in on the grade label.

## 3
Say: [confident] Under it, each acceptance criterion is marked met or not met, with the evidence. A file and line reference links straight to those lines in the merged code, so you can check it yourself.
Do: Pan down the criteria list and hover a file and line link.

## 4
Say: [thoughtful] The reasoning behind the grade comes last... If you think the review got something wrong, choose appeal this result.
Do: Hold on the reasoning paragraph, then click **Appeal this result**.

## 5
Say: [encouraging] Say SPECIFICALLY what it missed, and point at the work.
Do: Type "The review says no tests were added, but the new test file covers the new branch."

## 6
Say: [reassuring] Submit it. A person reads your appeal, not a model, and their decision is final. You'll see their reasoning here.
Do: Click **Submit appeal**. The result shows **Appeal under review**, with what you wrote.

## 7
Say: [serious] You can appeal only your own results, each one once, and only while the window is open. [pause] Once a person decides, the result says whether the appeal was upheld. Payouts aren't released until every appeal has been answered.
Do: Point at "Payouts are not released until every appeal has been answered."
