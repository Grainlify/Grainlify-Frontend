# What GrainHack is

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: contributor. Start: /dashboard?tab=osw

## 1
Say: [warmly] GrainHack is Grainlify's event programme. Each event has a sponsor-funded pool for contributors, and issues from the projects taking part.
Do: The GrainHack events list, **Live events**, is open. Pan down the events.

## 2
Say: [matter-of-fact] Every event shows its phase. Applications open means projects are applying to join, not YOU. Issue prep means their issues are being prepared.
Do: Point at the phase labels. Click the event in **Issue prep**; it says "Issues are being added". Click **All events**.

## 3
Say: [upbeat] When an event is live, its issues take applications. Each one shows its difficulty and how long is left to apply.
Do: Click the event marked **Live**. Its issue list loads, with the **Contributor pool** at the top.

## 4
Say: [calm] An issue joins an event when a maintainer of an accepted project adds the event's GrainHack label, and sets acceptance criteria and a difficulty.
Do: Hover an issue row, holding on its difficulty label.

## 5
Say: [confident] You don't get an issue by being first. [pause] When its window closes, a weighted draw picks who is assigned. Some issues are held for newcomers.
Do: Point at an issue's "left to apply" line, then at a **Newcomers only** label.

## 6
Say: [thoughtful] After the event closes, results are published and an appeal window opens... Then the event settles. Accepted work shares the event's pool.
Do: Click **All events**. Point at the events marked **Results published** and **Settled**.

## 7
Say: [friendly] Your applications, assignments, results, and the rules events run on are all in My GrainHack.
Do: Click **My GrainHack** in the rail. Pan across **My assignments**, **My applications**, **My results** and **Rules**.
