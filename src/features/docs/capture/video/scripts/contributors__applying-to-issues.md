# Apply for an issue

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: contributor. Start: /dashboard?tab=discover

## 1
Say: [warmly] On most issues, the project's maintainer chooses who works on it. You ask by applying, with a short message.
Do: On Discover, scroll down to **Recommended Issues**.

## 2
Say: [curious] Open an issue that interests you. A good first issue tag means the maintainer thinks it suits someone new to the project.
Do: Click an issue card tagged **good first issue** that mira-dev has not applied for. The issue page opens on the **Applications** tab.

## 3
Say: [matter-of-fact] You can apply when the issue is open, nobody is assigned to it, and you didn't open it yourself... Then you'll see Apply for this issue.
Do: Point at the apply box, then click **Apply for this issue**.

## 4
Say: [encouraging] Write a short message. Say how you'd tackle the issue, or point to similar work you've done. The dialog shows how your comment will begin.
Do: Type "I'd start by reproducing this with a failing test, then fix the parser. I fixed a similar bug in my own CLI last month." in the text box.

## 5
Say: [confident] Choose Submit application. Grainlify posts your message on the GitHub issue as a comment from your own account, so the maintainer sees it where they already work.
Do: Click **Submit application**. The dialog closes and the new application appears in the list.

## 6
Say: [reassuring] You'll get a notification, and the application appears under Applied on your Contributors tab, where you can follow it.
Do: Click **Contributors** in the rail. The **Contributions** board shows the application under **Applied**.

## 7
Say: [friendly] Um, changed your mind? Open the issue again, expand your application, and choose Withdraw. That deletes your comment from GitHub.
Do: Use the browser's back button to return to the issue. Click the arrow on the right of mira-dev's application to expand it. Point at **Withdraw** without clicking it.

## 8
Say: [calm] GrainHack issues work DIFFERENTLY. [pause] A draw decides who is assigned, so you apply for those from the GrainHack panel instead.
Do: Stay on the issue page. Fade out.
