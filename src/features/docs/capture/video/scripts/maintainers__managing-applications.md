# Review applications

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: maintainer. Start: /dashboard?tab=maintainers&view=maintainer&subtab=Issues

## 1
Say: [calm] When contributors apply for your issues, you decide who does the work. It all happens in the Issues tab of Maintainers, and Grainlify makes each change on GitHub for you.
Do: Show the **Issues** tab with the issue list on the left and **Select an issue** on the right.

## 2
Say: [friendly] Use the filter to find issues with applications that nobody has been given yet.
Do: Click the filter button next to **Search**. In **All Filters**, click **Yes** under **Applicants** and **No** under **Assignee**. Click **Apply**.

## 3
Say: [matter-of-fact] Open an issue. Its applications are listed with when each person applied... Expand one to read the message, or choose the name to see their profile.
Do: Click the issue #212 "Document the snapshot format" (3 applicants). On **Applications (3)**, click the arrow beside the first applicant to expand the message.

## 4
Say: [gentle] To turn someone down, choose Reject. Grainlify comments on the issue and lets them know, so they can move on.
Do: Expand the second application and click **Reject**.

## 5
Say: [confident] To give the issue to someone, choose Assign. Grainlify assigns them on GitHub, posts a comment asking them to link their pull request, and sends them a notification.
Do: Expand the first application again and click **Assign**. The applications show **Assigned**.

## 6
Say: [reassuring] Um, changed your mind, or the work has stalled? Unassign frees the issue on GitHub so you can choose someone else.
Do: Point at **Unassign** on the first application without clicking.

## 7
Say: [thoughtful] These buttons appear only in the maintainer view, on projects you own. [pause] Everyone else sees the applications without them.
Do: Click **CONTRIBUTOR** in the header. Go to **Browse**, **Repositories**, open tidewater-labs/ledgerline and open the same issue: the expanded application has no buttons. Click **MAINTAINER** to switch back.
