# Apply for a GrainHack issue

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: contributor. Start: /dashboard?tab=osw

## 1
Say: [friendly] To apply for a GrainHack issue, choose GrainHack in the rail, open a live event, and choose view issue on the issue you want.
Do: Click the event marked **Live**, then **View issue** on an issue whose window is open.

## 2
Say: [calm] The GrainHack panel at the top of the issue shows the event, the difficulty, how long is left to apply, and roughly how many people have applied.
Do: Zoom in on the panel, from **Part of** to the applicant count.

## 3
Say: [thoughtful] Read the acceptance criteria... If you win, your pull request is graded against them.
Do: Highlight the **Acceptance criteria** box.

## 4
Say: [warmly] You can add a note for the maintainer. The draw doesn't weight it.
Do: Click in **Anything you want to add (optional)** and type "I've worked on this module before and can start today."

## 5
Say: [confident] Choose apply for this issue. Applying doesn't use a slot. [pause] Only winning does. When the window closes, a weighted draw picks who is assigned, so applying early gives you NO advantage.
Do: Point at "Applying doesn't use a slot. Only winning does." Click **Apply for this issue**. The panel changes to "You've applied. The draw runs when the window closes."

## 6
Say: [matter-of-fact] Follow your applications in My GrainHack. Each one says whether it's waiting for the draw, won, or not selected.
Do: Click **My GrainHack** in the rail, then **My applications**.

## 7
Say: [reassuring] Uh, if a check stopped you, the application says it couldn't apply, and tells you exactly which rule and why.
Do: Point at a row marked **Couldn't apply** and its reason.

## 8
Say: [pleased] When you win, you get a notification, and the issue moves to your assignments.
Do: Click **My assignments**.
