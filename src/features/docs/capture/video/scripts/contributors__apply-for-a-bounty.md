# Apply for a bounty

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: contributor with a linked wallet. Start: /dashboard?tab=bounties

## 1
Say: [warmly] Grainlify bounties aren't first come, first served. You apply while a bounty's window is open, and when it closes, a weighted draw picks one person.
Do: The Bounties page is open. Scroll to the open bounties list.

## 2
Say: [matter-of-fact] Each open bounty says when applications close, and roughly how many people have applied. The exact count stays hidden until the window closes, so there's nothing to time.
Do: Zoom in on an open bounty's line, "Applications close in … A few applicants so far."

## 3
Say: [friendly] You can, uh, add a short note if you like. The draw doesn't weight it.
Do: Click in **Anything you want to add (optional)** and type "I fixed a similar parser bug in this repository last month."

## 4
Say: [confident] Choose apply for this bounty. You're now in the draw... Don't open a pull request YET.
Do: Click **Apply for this bounty**. The row changes to "You are in the draw for this bounty. The result appears here when it runs."

## 5
Say: [calm] When the window closes, one ticket is drawn. Everyone starts with one. [pause] Newcomers get a bonus, past wins add more but only the first two count, and abandoned bounties cost you. The live weights are on the public rules page.
Do: Go to /bounties/rules. Hold on the "Prior wins are capped at" card, then scroll through the **Weights** table.

## 6
Say: [reassuring] Just as important is what the draw can't see: your pull request count, merge rate, followers, stars, total contributions, or how well your application is written.
Do: Scroll back up to **What the draw cannot see**.

## 7
Say: [encouraging] Some bounties are marked first bounty only. They're drawn from people who haven't completed a bounty yet.
Do: Go back to /dashboard?tab=bounties and point at the row marked **First bounty only**.

## 8
Say: [upbeat] After the draw, every row shows who holds the bounty. If it's you, your row says so, and the next video covers what to do.
Do: Point at a row that says "Assigned to …".
