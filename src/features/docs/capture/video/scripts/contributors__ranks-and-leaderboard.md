# Ranks and the leaderboard

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: contributor (mira-dev), ranked on the season and all-time boards, with at least two active ecosystems in the fixture. Start: /dashboard?tab=discover

## 1
Say: [friendly] The leaderboard ranks contributors by pull requests merged into projects listed on Grainlify. Open it from Leaderboard in the rail.
Do: Click **Leaderboard** in the rail. The **Seasonal Contributors** heading and the podium load.

## 2
Say: [matter-of-fact] It opens on this season, which means merges in the last ninety days, always counted back from today. [pause] Each merged pull request counts as one. Open pull requests, issues, reviews and bots don't count.
Do: Hover **This season** in the period toggle, then scroll down to the table.

## 3
Say: [calm] Switch to All time to count every merge on record, under the same rules.
Do: Click **All time**. The heading changes to **All-Time Contributors**. Then click **This season** again.

## 4
Say: [upbeat] Your position places you in a tier. The top five are Conqueror, then Ace, Crown, Diamond, Gold and Silver, down to Bronze... With no merges in the window, you're unranked.
Do: Click mira-dev's row in the table. Their profile opens.

## 5
Say: [warmly] Your profile shows your tier and position this season, with your all-time standing on the line beneath.
Do: Zoom on the rank card, including the "All time" line.

## 6
Say: [curious] The Projects toggle ranks organisations instead, by how many different people had work merged there.
Do: Click **Leaderboard** in the rail, then click **Projects** at the top. The heading changes to **Top Projects**.

## 7
Say: [thoughtful] The ecosystem filter limits either board to one ecosystem, and positions are worked out again for that ecosystem alone.
Do: Click **All Ecosystems** and choose the first ecosystem in the list. The board reloads.

## 8
Say: [reassuring] Rank is a public record of your work, not money. [pause] It doesn't change your chances in a draw, and it doesn't affect ANY payout.
Do: Hold on the filtered board.
