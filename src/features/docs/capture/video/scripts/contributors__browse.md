# Browse projects and organizations

Length target: 60-90 seconds. Persona: contributor. Start: /dashboard?tab=discover

## 1
Say: [friendly] Browse lists the projects on Grainlify, newest first. You'll find it in the rail on the left.
Do: Click **Browse** in the rail. Browse opens on the **Organizations** view.

## 2
Say: [calm] It opens on Organizations. Each card is one GitHub organisation, with how many of its repositories are listed, its stars and its contributors.
Do: Move the pointer slowly across the first two organisation cards.

## 3
Say: [curious] Switch to Repositories to see every project on its own card, with its open issues and pull requests.
Do: Click **Repositories**. Scroll down a little so a full row of repository cards is visible, then back up.

## 4
Say: [matter-of-fact] To narrow the list, pick what to filter by, here the language. Open the list, search for a value, and tick it... The grid updates straight away.
Do: Click **Language**. Click **Select languages**. Type "type" in **Search languages...**. Tick **TypeScript**. Click the cross in the list's header to close it.

## 5
Say: [warmly] Each filter you set shows as a chip at the top. You can add more from Ecosystem, Category or Tag, and remove one with its cross.
Do: Point at the **TypeScript** chip above the toggle. Click its cross. The full list returns.

## 6
Say: [encouraging] Choose a repository to open its project page, with its readme, its open issues and its recent activity.
Do: Click the first repository card. On the project page, scroll to **Issues**, then to **Recent Activity**.

## 7
Say: [amused] [chuckles] Back to Browse takes you back. [pause] In Organizations, a card opens the organisation's page instead, which lists all of its repositories on Grainlify.
Do: Click **Back to Browse**. Browse reopens on **Organizations**. Click the first organisation card. Scroll to **Repositories**.

## 8
Say: [pleased] Pick a repository there and you're on its project page, ready to find an issue to work on.
Do: Click the first repository under **Repositories**. The project page opens.
